Privacy Policy

Lost Lake Disc Club · Last updated October 24, 2026

Lost Lake Disc Club is a disc golf game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: the game does not collect, sell or share your personal information, and it has no ads and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and the developer does not run any servers that receive data from the game.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and game progress (for example your scores, level and discs) are stored on your device so the game can remember them. This data stays on your device unless you use Apple services described below.

Apple services

The game may use Apple services such as Game Center (leaderboards), iCloud (syncing your progress between your own devices) and the App Store (purchases). When you use them, Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer only sees what Apple makes available to developers, such as leaderboard scores and Game Center nicknames.

If you test the game through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, device and usage information, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with the developer, to help fix problems. See Apple's TestFlight terms for details.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game shows no ads, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/ColeoCofer/lost-lake-disc-club/issues.
