Lost Lake Disc Club Support

Need help or want to report a problem? Open a support issue. Include your game version, device model and what happened. A screenshot helps.

TestFlight feedback

Friends testing the game can send feedback and screenshots through TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option.

Progress and saves

The game saves progress locally. With iCloud enabled, it can also sync progress between your devices using the same Apple Account. If progress appears late on another device, reconnect to the internet and reopen the game to allow iCloud to sync.

Online rankings

Sign in to Game Center to post a ranked card. Offline Career remains available; offline rounds can earn stars without posting a ranked result.

The full-game purchase

The public game has one optional full-game unlock. If you already bought it, use Restore Purchases in Settings with the same App Store account. The purchase restriction is off while friends test through TestFlight.

Daily courses

Courses refresh at 08:00 UTC. A round belongs to the day it started. Finish before the next reset to post a final ranked result. Your first counted card is used for the day's rankings; replays can improve your stars.
